About

Medical Card keeps the medical documents of your whole family in one place. Add a PDF, a photo, a scan or plain text. An AI model files it by person, date and medical area, then writes a summary and a translation.

Export by person, area and period as a PDF report in the language you choose, or as a full ZIP archive.

The original file always stays in the app, so you can check what the AI read. The archive lives on your device. There is no server and no account.

What it does

  • Add anything

    PDFs, photos, scans, emails and text, or share a file from another app.

  • Several people

    Keep records for the whole family, each under their own name.

  • Summary and translation

    Every document gets a short summary and a translation into your language.

  • Ask your archive

    Ask in plain words and get an answer built from your own documents.

  • You stay in control

    Check what the AI read, mark it as checked or correct it. The original is always kept.

  • Export

    Share a PDF report with a doctor in the language you choose, or save a full ZIP archive.

PRIVACY

Your documents stay yours.

Medical Card has no server and no account. Your archive stays on your device, and the app sends nothing anywhere until you agree.

  • No account, no server, no analytics.
  • The archive stays on your device.
  • You choose the AI service: ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Claude (Anthropic). Only requests to that service leave the device, and only after you consent.
  • You use your own API key. The AI provider charges you for the requests: about $0.01 to $0.04 for each document.
  • Optional Face ID lock.

AI can make mistakes. Medical Card is not medical advice.
